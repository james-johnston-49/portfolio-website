My name is James Johnston, and I am a creative individual with a passion for art, storytelling, and design. Over the years I have developed a strong interest in digital illustration, animation, coding, and game development. I enjoy exploring new creative challenges and constantly learning skills that allow me to bring ideas to life. Whether I am creating artwork, designing websites, or experimenting with game concepts, I am always looking for opportunities to improve and express my creativity. I believe that curiosity and continuous learning are some of the most valuable tools an artist can have. Through this portfolio, I hope to share my work, document my growth, and connect with others who are passionate about creativity and innovation.
